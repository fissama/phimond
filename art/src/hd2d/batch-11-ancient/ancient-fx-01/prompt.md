# Ancient FX 01 — drifting jade crystal motes

Four-frame 128×128 horizontal sprite strip of sparse small emerald crystal motes drifting/fading. Generated with built-in image_gen and Ancient crystal reference. Raw contained a neutral checker matte; removed low-saturation background pixels, binary alpha thresholded, tightly cropped each cell, nearest-scaled to 72px maximum and centered; jointly quantized to a shared 48-color palette.
