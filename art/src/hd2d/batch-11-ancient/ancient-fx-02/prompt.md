# Ancient FX 02 — limestone rubble burst

Four-frame horizontal 128×128 strip. Scattered limestone/jade grit expands into a larger rubble burst, then settles to small stones. Generated by built-in image_gen with Ancient decal and wall references. Blue matte removed using chroma key, then each cell was cropped, nearest-scaled, centered, and jointly quantized to 48 colors. Raw and original study candidate retained.
