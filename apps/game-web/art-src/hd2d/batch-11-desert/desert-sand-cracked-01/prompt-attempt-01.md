Phimond desert terrain texture production SHEET, FOUR genuinely different floor textures in2columns x2rows. Image1 ORIGINAL APK desert map content (warm dry sand, dunes, eroded stone); images2 approved batch01 grass floor and3 batch02 city paving are STYLE references for readable clustered game pixel texture, notcopytheirpatterns. Images4/5 pet anchors for tonal depth only, do not draw creatures.
Sheet1536x1536, four square tiles each640x640, centered in its768x768cell,64px #FF00FF gutters/outer margins. Every tile a PERFECT SQUARE with straight bounds, full opaque texture inside. Background only outside tile is flatmagenta. NO labels/borders/UI/objects/shadows/perspective. Orthographic ground material.
Top LEFT desert-sand-floor-01: long gentle sinuous parallel WIND RIPPLES, broken naturally, fine scattered tiny warm gray/ivory/redstone flecks, broad readable pixel clusters. Flat base #D9B26B.
Top RIGHT desert-sand-floor-02: SHORT crosswind herringbone/curled dune ripplelets in irregular small groups with broad quiet gaps and small dry pebble grit. Completely different stroke directions, spacing and placement fromtile1, NOT a recolor or shifted tile1. Same base #D9B26B.
Bottom LEFT desert-sandstone-floor: irregular warm red sandstone pavers/slabs, chipped edges and mottled warmfaces, subdued cool mauve joints, weathered sand grains. Different masonry piece sizes and worn surfaces, notregulargrid. Base #A5522B.
Bottom RIGHT desert-sand-cracked-01: parched ochre clay clods, irregular polygonal cracks with cool gray-violet depth, little sandy infill and a few small ochre/ivory gravelchips. No dunes/ripples. Base #B88A44.
CRITICAL SEAM: outermost16px on ALL FOUR SIDES of each tile must be perfectly flat SOLID exact BASE color with no details/no shadows. Leftedge=rightedge=topedge=bottomedge pixel-for-pixel. All detail at least16px away from boundary, no vignette or frame line. Let texture density taper through sparse clusters rather than a rectangular shaded frame.
Palette restrained Octopath-like exactly same contrast as approved batch01/02: sand#D9B26B/#B88A44, sandstone#A5522B, cream limestone chips, muted mauve-gray cool creases. Rich weathering but deliberate2-4tone clusters per material,33-48colors per tile afterfinish, no noise/dither/blur/smoothgradients or oil painting. No black outlines; darkumber/coolgray material creases. Distinct materials, do not tint everything one brown. Full48color kit palette will be applied after extraction.
