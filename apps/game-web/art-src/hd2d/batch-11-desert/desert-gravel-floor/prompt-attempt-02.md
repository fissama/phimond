Route: built-in image_gen. Model not exposed.

A single seamless square gameplay ground tile,1024x1024, Phimond desert gravel. Reference1 original desert CONTENT/color; approved grass2 and paving3 PIXEL STYLE; pets4–5 outline/value only, no creatures.
Full square opaque top-down warm ochre sand base #B88A44 with evenly scattered SMALL ANGULAR PEBBLES of rust red sandstone, pale ivory limestone and cool gray-mauve, interspersed tiny weathered dark brown wood chips and olive dry lichen grains. Multiple distinct mineral materials, 33–48 colors after palette finish. Small5–18pixel pieces in the intended512px tile, flushed into compacted dust, no raised large boulders or cast shadows. No long lines or cracks, no stripes, no central mass. Uniform detail density across whole image to within16pixels of edge. No tile frame.
The OUTERMOST16pixels on all FOUR sides (including all corners) must be PERFECTLY UNIFORM #B88A44, no gradient, no specks, no shadow/bevel. This is a VERY NARROW invisible seamless gutter only, not a wide blank border. Everything after that16px must have the same pebble density as the center so repeating3x3 makes no square grid. Opposing edge pixels exactly identical base color.
Deliberate crisp squared pixel clusters, restrained textures,2–4tones per material, warm upper-left local facets and cool underside. Exact approved batch00–09 artist / Octopath inspired HD2D. No smooth oilpaint, gradients, dither/noise, fog, bloom, object sprites, letters or magenta. This is1square tile, no presentation canvas or labels.
