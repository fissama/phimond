# Batch 3 — Beach & Coastal Kit (Octopath HD-2D)

Coverage for 5 Coastal Maps (Fishermen Beach, East Beach, West Beach, Deep Sea, Prosperity Island):
- Terrain: dry warm sand, dry cool sand, wet tide sand, seagrass sand, shore foam animations
- Props: palm trees (sway-split canopy/trunk variations 1–3), coastal shore boulders (large, medium, small), sea shells (clam, conch, scallop, spiral), coral starfish (ochre, coral), driftwood logs, wooden pier deck sections & pilings, fishing boats (hull 1 & 2), drying net racks, fisherman huts, coastal stone lighthouse, treasure chests (closed & open)
- Deep sea: brain coral, branching coral, tube coral, undersea kelp, animated rising bubbles
- Backdrops: distant open sea with archipelago islands, coastal rocky cliffs
